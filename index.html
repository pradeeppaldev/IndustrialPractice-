Featured Artists

Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch painter known for his bold, dramatic brush strokes, which expressed emotion and added a feeling of movement to his works. He is one of the most influential figures in the history of Western art.

Notable Works: Starry Night, Sunflowers, The Bedroom

Pablo Picasso

Picasso was a Spanish painter and sculptor who co-founded the Cubist movement. His work revolutionized the visual arts with an exploration of different perspectives and fragmented forms.

Notable Works: Guernica, Les Demoiselles d'Avignon, The Weeping Woman
